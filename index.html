Help / FAQ Section

You have questions? I have the answers.

Incomplete Resources and related topics

If there's an error or incomplete information related to one of the resources, contact me on my Twitter or Discord.


Missing Resources or Player References

If you want to add a resource or a player name, please fill this form.


Player card not updated

I know and I'm sorry about that. I'm only one person trying to collect results from every tournament from every corner of the world.
Contact me on my Twitter or Discord and I'll gladly fix that. Thanks in advance.


What's your criteria for player references?

I try to include players from different regions for reference.

Priority goes to players that got actual results from tournaments, even if the are only regional events.

Old players will not appear. Exception goes to characters that didn't come close to those results with newer players.


Suggestions and Feedbacks

If you got an idea on how to make this website even better, please fill this form.


Why did you make this website?

Resources for Melty Blood: Type Lumina are all scattered and not well organized.

Mizuumi Wiki is not updated properly, specially on the player references (please update the wiki with the player data presented here).

And most importantly: I want to make something cool to the community that I like so much. You're awesome.
